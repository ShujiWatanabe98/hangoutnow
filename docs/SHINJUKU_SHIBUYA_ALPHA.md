# Shinjuku / Shibuya Invite Alpha

## Scope

- Adults (18+) invited by the operations team only.
- Areas: Shinjuku and Shibuya station areas.
- Initial activities: ramen, walking, and beginner running.
- Meeting points must be public, staffed, and easy to leave. Homes, hotel rooms,
  private rooms, and vehicles are not allowed.
- Exact coordinates stay hidden until a host accepts a join request.

## Daily supply

Operations should publish at least three verified-host Hangouts per active area
and time window before inviting participants. Do not open a new area while its
existing feed regularly has no viable Hangouts.

Recommended templates:

1. Ramen in 30 minutes, 2-4 people.
2. 30-minute walk, first-timers welcome.
3. Conversational-pace 3 km run, beginners welcome.

## Launch gates

- Two real devices and two accounts complete create -> join -> accept -> chat ->
  finish -> feedback.
- Report, block, suspension, and audit-history flows are verified.
- Capacity cannot be exceeded by concurrent approvals.
- Notifications cover join requests, decisions, and the pre-start reminder.
- Production privacy, terms, community guidelines, deletion, and support links
  resolve under `method-more.com`.

## Metrics

The north-star metric is weekly safely completed Hangouts. Review these funnel
events by area and activity:

- discover view
- Hangout detail view
- join request
- join accepted or rejected
- chat opened
- Hangout finished
- feedback submitted

Also review time to first request, 30/60/180-minute match rate, attendance,
no-show and late-cancellation rate, seven-day repeat participation/hosting,
report rate, and moderation response time.

## Stop conditions

Pause new invitations for an area when a high-risk report is unresolved, the
moderation owner is unavailable, or the public feed cannot provide enough
verified-host supply for the announced window.
